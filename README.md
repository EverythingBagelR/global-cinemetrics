**Film genres, historical context, and audience reception — a collaborative digital humanities project.**

[Explore the live project](https://globalcinemetrics.humspace.ucla.edu/) · [Meet the team](https://globalcinemetrics.humspace.ucla.edu/about/meet-the-team/) · [中文介绍](README.zh-CN.md)

## Project overview

Global Cinemetrics examines how film genre patterns and audience responses relate to social change, historical events, and cultural context. The project brings together movie metadata from TMDB and Letterboxd, visual analysis, historical interpretation, and a public research website hosted on UCLA HumSpace.

The research explores patterns across time and countries, including the United States and the effects of COVID-19. Its central question is how shifts in cinema connect to the societies that produce and receive films.

## Data and publishing approach

The team's published methods describe normalizing movie titles, joining the TMDB and Letterboxd datasets by title, distinguishing overlapping fields, and removing rows with missing values. The website uses WordPress with the Film Maker Lite theme and is hosted on UCLA HumSpace.

The [data critique](https://globalcinemetrics.humspace.ucla.edu/data/) discusses platform and language bias, incomplete metadata, and the limits of treating engagement metrics as cultural significance. These considerations shape how the project interprets its findings.
